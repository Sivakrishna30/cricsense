# CricSense Terms And Transparency

## Product Stance

CricSense is an analytics app.

It is not:
- a guaranteed-winning fantasy service
- an investment promise
- a betting or profit assurance tool

It is:
- a recent-form and performance analysis layer
- a context and instinct-based match helper
- a fantasy team support tool

## Core Model

CricSense uses:
- `stats layer`
- `context layer`
- `instinct layer`

At runtime it also uses:
- venue
- squad
- pitch note
- dew
- rain chance
- toss winner
- toss decision

## Captain And Vice-Captain

Captain and vice-captain suggestions are generated from current model outputs:
- runtime score
- stability score
- upside score

These suggestions are guidance only.
Users should still apply their own instinct before finalizing fantasy teams.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:
- how they interpret the analysis
- whether they enter any contest
- whether they change teams, captain, or vice-captain

CricSense does not guarantee winnings or contest outcomes.
